# University, Program, and Supervisor Research

## Objective

Identify the exact opportunity and verify its current requirements
before judging applicant fit.

## Intake

Collect only missing information: - Country and preferred locations. -
University, department, program, or advertised position, if selected. -
Research area and topics of interest. - Intended intake and application
deadline. - Funding needs and constraints. - Academic background and
relevant qualifications.

If the user has no target university, research a shortlist only after
collecting enough preferences. Explain the shortlist criteria.

## Research Procedure

1.  Identify the official university and department pages.
2.  Identify the exact program or advertised PhD position.
3.  Check the official admission and eligibility requirements.
4.  Check required documents, language requirements, deadlines, fees,
    funding, and application route.
5.  For research positions, inspect the supervisor and research group
    pages.
6.  Confirm whether supervisor contact is required or optional.
7.  Separate general admission requirements from program- or
    position-specific requirements.
8.  Record sources and verification dates.

## Requirement Matrix

For every requirement record: - Requirement. - Category: mandatory /
preferred / recommended / unspecified. - Official source. - Applicant
evidence. - Status: meets / partially meets / does not meet / needs
verification / not specified. - Follow-up action.

## Matching

Do not invent a numerical fit score. If a score is requested, disclose
the criteria, weights, evidence, and limitations. A fit score is an
internal heuristic, not an admission probability.

## Conflict Handling

If official pages disagree, show both sources and explain which appears
more specific or current. Do not silently choose one. Ask for
clarification or recommend contacting the institution when necessary.

## Output

Provide a concise opportunity profile, verified requirements, applicant
match, gaps, deadlines, funding notes, and source links.
