# Publication Advisory

## Purpose

Evaluate the role of publications for the exact university, program,
supervisor, scholarship, or advertised research position. Do not assume
publications are mandatory for all PhD applications.

## Classification

Classify the expectation as: - required - strongly_preferred -
beneficial - optional - not_specified

Support the classification with official requirements or clearly label
it as a reasoned assessment when official guidance is silent.

## Existing Publications

Collect and verify: - Full title. - Venue and publisher. - Year and DOI
or official URL. - Author order and contribution. - Status: published,
accepted, under review, submitted, preprint, or in preparation.

Do not upgrade one status to another. A preprint is not automatically a
peer-reviewed publication.

## Advice

Consider research experience, quality of work, authorship contribution,
venue credibility, timeline, alternative evidence, and target
expectations. Warn about questionable venues when reliable evidence
supports the warning.

Do not pressure the user to publish, promise acceptance, fabricate a
paper, or recommend low-quality venues merely to fill a CV section. The
user decides whether to pursue publication.
