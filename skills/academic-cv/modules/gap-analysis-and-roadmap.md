# Gap Analysis and Three-Month Roadmap

## Gap Analysis

Compare verified target requirements with the user's profile.

Statuses: - meets - partially_meets - does_not_meet -
needs_verification - not_specified

Distinguish: - Documentation gap: the user may have the
skill/experience, but evidence or CV description is missing. -
Capability gap: reliable information confirms the user needs to develop
the skill. - Eligibility gap: a formal requirement is not met. -
Research-fit gap: the applicant's interests or experience do not yet
align strongly with the target.

## Prioritization

Prioritize based on: - Mandatory vs preferred status. - Importance to
the target. - Deadline. - Existing capability and evidence. -
Dependencies. - Feasibility within available time.

Do not rank a preferred publication above an unmet mandatory eligibility
requirement without a clear reason.

## Roadmap Intake

Before planning, establish: - Target university/program/position and
deadline. - Current academic stage and relevant subject-wise
performance. - Research interests and preferred topics. - Existing
skills, projects, and experience. - Current responsibilities and weekly
available hours. - Hardware, software, budget, access, and other
constraints. - User's preferred learning style or working constraints
when relevant.

Ask only for missing information that materially affects the plan.

## Independent Research and Planning

Use available research tools to investigate current requirements,
learning resources, relevant methods, and research trends. Verify
important claims. The AI may recommend additional skills, but must
explain why they matter to the target.

## Roadmap Task Format

Each task should contain: - Task. - Rationale. - Priority. - Estimated
duration. - Dependencies. - Deliverable. - Completion criteria. -
Source/evidence where relevant.

Provide a realistic week-by-week or phase-based three-month plan, with
buffer time and explicit assumptions. Durations are estimates, not
guarantees.

## Progress

Use completed, in_progress, pending, blocked, and needs_verification.
Mark a task completed only when the user or evidence confirms it.

## Report

Separate current strengths, completed requirements, open gaps,
recommended actions, timeline estimates, and remaining uncertainties.
