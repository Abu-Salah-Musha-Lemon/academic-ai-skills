# FAQ

### Does this make AI writing undetectable?
No. The project does not attempt to evade detection. It aims for authentic, accurate, clear writing that reflects the user's own information and voice.

### Are citations guaranteed to be correct?
No. Skills require verification, but AI and search tools can make mistakes. Check every source and citation before relying on it.

### Can I use this for any field or country?
The skills are general-purpose. Adapt discipline-specific methods, application conventions, and local requirements to your context.

### Are the skills tested with a real model?
Static tests are automated. Model behavior test cases are provided, but real model runs are separate and must be documented.

### Can the AI write a recommendation letter?
It can help prepare a factual briefing or draft where permitted. The recommender must independently review, approve, and submit/sign it.

### What information should I provide?
Provide relevant facts and application requirements. Avoid sharing passwords, national identifiers, or unrelated sensitive information.
